What explains the differences between wells

Share of the well-to-well spread in each outcome (first 12 months, per 1,000 ft of lateral) that one factor accounts for, adjusted for how many groups it has. Each factor is tested alone; the last two rows combine them.

These tests always cover both operators and every formation together, so they do not change with the formation setting.
